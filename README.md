## ✨ About

**Wang Yi's 3D Celestial World** is a small interactive 3D birthday world created specially for **Wang Yi's 17th birthday**.

Instead of being a traditional birthday webpage, the project turns the birthday message into a small fantasy adventure.

You can explore floating islands, discover hidden locations, collect stars, interact with flowers and mushrooms, watch shooting stars, solve small puzzles, open a mysterious memory box, and eventually discover a personal birthday letter.

> 🌌 **It's not just a birthday page.**
>
> It's a tiny world made for one person.

---

# 🌷 Features

## 🌌 Star Island

A floating celestial island hidden above the clouds.

### ⭐ Giant Star Tree

At the center of the island stands a giant magical tree.

- Interactive star branches
- Glowing crystal foliage
- Golden star lanterns
- Wind animation
- Ambient particles
- Interactive messages
- Bunny reactions

Interact with the tree to discover hidden secrets.

---

## 🌙 Moon Platform

A crescent-shaped platform overlooking the clouds.

When the player reaches the platform:

- The moon becomes brighter
- The sky changes
- Meteor showers appear
- A special wish interaction becomes available

> **"Make a wish, Wang Yi."**

---

## ✨ Shooting Stars

Shooting stars randomly cross the night sky.

Follow them and discover where they land.

Some shooting stars become **Wish Stars** that can be collected by the bunny.

Collecting special stars can reward:

```text
+25 Birthday Flower Points
```

# 🔮 Secret Island

A hidden island that can only be accessed after completing the required objectives.

The atmosphere changes completely:

**Star Island**

> ✨ Magical • Adventurous • Celestial

**Secret Island**

> 🌙 Quiet • Dreamy • Emotional • Mysterious

---

## 🌷 Memory Garden

A peaceful garden containing special **Memory Flowers**.

Explore the area and discover hidden memory fragments.

Collect them all to unlock the next part of the story.

---

## 🍄 Glow Mushroom Forest

A mysterious forest filled with glowing mushrooms.

Interacting with the mushrooms creates:

- Teal particle bursts
- Magical light
- Hidden interactions
- Puzzle elements

---

## 💎 Crystal Waterfall

A glowing waterfall flows from the floating island into the clouds.

The waterfall contains a hidden secret.

Discovering the correct interaction reveals a hidden path.

---

## 📦 Memory Box

Behind the waterfall lies a hidden cave.

Inside is a mysterious **Memory Box**.

The box requires the collected memory fragments to unlock.

Opening it triggers a cinematic sequence.

---

# 💌 Birthday Letter

One of the main experiences of the project.

The letter opens through a multi-stage cinematic animation:

```text
1. Floating Envelope
        ↓
2. Wax Seal Awakens
        ↓
3. Envelope Opens
        ↓
4. Golden Light Burst
        ↓
5. Letter Rises
        ↓
6. Typewriter Message
```

The final message reveals:

> **Happy 17th Birthday, Wang Yi. 🐰🌷✨**

The environment reacts to the message.

Flowers bloom.

Butterflies rise.

Fireflies gather.

The waterfall begins to glow.

The night sky changes.

And distant fireworks appear.

### IDLE

- Ear movement

### EXCITED

- Looking toward the sky

### HAPPY

- Spinning

- Excited movement

The bunny can:

- Chase shooting stars
- Collect Wish Stars
- Look at the moon
